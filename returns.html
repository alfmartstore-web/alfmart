Returns & Exchange

Your satisfaction is our priority. We offer a straightforward return and exchange process to ensure a confident shopping experience.

Our Policy

We accept returns within 7 days of the delivery date. To be eligible for a return, your item must be unused, in the same condition that you received it, and in its original packaging.

Exchanges are available for different sizes or colors of the same product, subject to availability.

Non-returnable items:

  • Gift cards
  • Personalized or custom items
  • Items marked as 'Final Sale'

To initiate a return, please contact our support team with your order number and reason for return.

The Process

1

Initiate Return

Contact us via email or WhatsApp with your order details.

2

Schedule Pickup

We'll arrange a free pickup from your address through our courier partners.

3

Inspection

Once we receive the item, we'll inspect it to ensure it meets the return criteria.

4

Refund or Exchange

A full refund is issued to your original payment method or a new item is dispatched.
